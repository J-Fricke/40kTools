LEAGUES OF VOTANN
ARMY RULE: YIELD POINTS
Source: Codex Leagues of Votann, 11th edition (via warhammer-community.com,
"Maximise your yields with the new Leagues of Votann army rule")

YIELD POINTS
At the end of each player's Command phase, you gain 1YP if you control one
or more objective markers within your deployment zone, and, from the second
battle round onwards, you gain 1 additional YP for each of the following
conditions you satisfy:
- You control one or more objective markers not within your deployment zone.
- You control two or more objective markers not within your deployment zone.
- You control more objective markers than your opponent.
You also gain 1 YP each time you destroy an enemy unit through the Martial
Leverage rule.

YP is a genuinely two-layer resource, not just a passive threshold trigger -
both layers below matter and are separate from each other.

LAYER 1: PASSIVE THRESHOLD ABILITY (not spent, just checked)
Depending on your current YP total, every unit in your army has one of the
following two abilities. Both are CONDITIONAL on objective-marker proximity,
not an unconditional bonus - the app's "+1 Hit" / "No YP" toggle simplifies
this to a binary assume-active/assume-inactive switch, which is a reasonable
approximation for a quick calculator but not literally what the rule says.

HOSTILE ACQUISITION (while you have fewer than 7 YP)
- Each time a model in this unit makes an attack that targets an enemy unit
  within range of one or more objective markers, add 1 to the Hit roll.
- You can re-roll Advance and Charge rolls made for this unit.

FORTIFY TAKEOVER (while you have 7 or more YP)
- Each time a model in this unit makes an attack that targets an enemy unit,
  if this unit is within range of one or more objective markers you control,
  add 1 to the Hit roll.
- Each time an attack targets this unit, if the Strength characteristic of
  that attack is greater than this unit's Toughness characteristic and this
  unit is not a Vehicle, subtract 1 from the Wound roll.

LAYER 2: SPENDABLE RESOURCE (separate mechanic, NOT modeled anywhere in the
app currently - the "yp" toggle only covers Layer 1 above)
Whenever a rule lets you spend YP, your YP total is reduced by that amount
(cannot go below 0); if you don't have enough, you can't use that rule.
Known examples (paraphrased from secondary sources, not independently
verified against the Codex text - re-check exact wording before relying on
specifics):
- Ancestral Sentence (stratagem): gives a unit Sustained Hits 1 in the
  Shooting phase; spending 3 YP upgrades that to Sustained Hits 2.
- A movement stratagem: lets a unit Advance and Charge as its baseline
  effect; spending up to 2 YP adds extra inches of movement scaled to YP
  spent.
- Uthar the Destined's Ancestral Fortune: once per turn, spend 1 YP to
  change a hit/wound/save roll to an unmodified 6.
