PRIMARY MISSION CARDS — transcribed from ref/all-the-primary-mission-cards-*.webp photos.
Icon legend (confirmed by user against physical cards):
  Shield/skull badge = TAKE AND HOLD
  Eye                = RECONNAISSANCE
  Target/compass     = PRIORITY ASSETS
  X                  = DISRUPTION
  Sword-in-triangle  = PURGE THE FOE

These are PRIMARY mission cards — the primary mission is determined by the
disposition matchup itself, not drawn/dealt. (SECONDARY missions are a separate,
similarly-styled deck, NOT covered by this file — different draw-2 mechanic,
different card pool; don't conflate the two.)

Card anatomy: top-left = primary mission name/spine. Lower-left icon = the card's
OWN disposition (which 5-card pool it belongs to — this is what each section below
is organized by). Middle = scoring rules. Lower-right icon (next to "OPPONENT") =
the opponent disposition that triggers use of this specific card — within your own
disposition's 5-card pool, which one of the 5 you actually play depends on what
your opponent is running that game. Each pool has exactly 5 unique missions (one
per possible opponent disposition, including a mirror matchup vs itself), with one
of the 5 printed as 2 physical copies for some reason (not a 6th mission).

Priority Assets is structurally different: its 6 cards (5 unique + Sabotage x2)
have NO opponent-matchup badge at all — just a plain Objective Action icon. All are
"Objective Action" cards (secure/maintain/extract/vanguard/sabotage) with no VP
scoring table printed on them. Confirmed this is the correct/complete PA image, so
PA's scoring is likely defined by a shared/generic rule elsewhere (check
core-rules.txt or event-companion-v1.1.txt) rather than per-card — not yet found.

Transcribed from photos, not official text — minor OCR errors possible in VP
numbers/wording. All 4 non-PA sections below are now fully cross-referenced
(every card's opponent-matchup confirmed against a clear photo).

RECONNAISSANCE (eye) — full 5-matchup set confirmed
================================================================================
Recon missions consistently reward: units spread across multiple table quarters,
completing actions (intel/markers), and denying/clearing zones of enemy models —
NOT raw kill count or single-objective camping.

RECONNAISSANCE SWEEP (vs Take and Hold)
  Any battle round: 1+ friendly units in 3+ different table quarters and not within
    6" of centre of battlefield — 3VP; for each enemy unit destroyed this turn — 1VP
  2nd round onwards: you control 1+ objectives (excl. home) — 3VP

TRIANGULATION (vs Purge the Foe, Objective Action)
  Starts: your Shooting phase, 2nd round onwards. Units: 1 friendly unit within
    range of an objective. Use limit: once/turn. Completes: end of turn if your unit
    and one other unit are within 6" of that objective. Effect: objective is
    "triangulated."
  2nd round onwards: 2 objectives triangulated — 6VP; 3+ triangulated — 10VP

SURVEIL THE FOE (vs Disruption, Objective Action)
  Starts: your Shooting phase. Units: any friendly unit. Use limit: once/unit.
    Completes: immediately. Effect: select a visible enemy unit not yet surveilled
    this turn; it is surveilled until end of the battle round.

GATHER INTEL (vs Reconnaissance — mirror matchup, Objective Action)
  Starts: your Shooting phase. Units: 1 friendly unit within range of an objective
    (each unit starting this action must use a different objective). Use limit:
    once/turn. Completes: end of turn if your unit controls that objective.
    Effect: place an operation marker within range of that objective.
  Scoring seen: 4VP control-based; 7VP for completing the Extract Intelligence
    action; 5VP end-of-battle for 3+ operation markers on the battlefield / a
    marker within range of opponent's home objective.
  (Two near-identical copies photographed — the x2-print pattern, not two
  separate missions.)

SEARCH AND SCOUR (vs Priority Assets)
  Any battle round: control 1+ central objectives — 3VP; 1+ enemy units that
    started the turn within a terrain area are destroyed — 2VP
  2nd round onwards: for each objective you control (excl. home) — 4VP
  End of battle: no enemy units wholly within your territory — 5VP

================================================================================
DISRUPTION (X) — full 5-matchup set confirmed
================================================================================
Disruption missions lean on terrain/objective denial, decoys, and operation markers
rather than pure kill count or simple hold.

DEATH TRAP (vs Take and Hold)
  Any battle round: for each terrain area trapped this turn — 2VP (+3VP cumulative
    if it's an objective); 1+ enemy units that started the turn in a trapped
    terrain area were destroyed — 3VP
  2nd round onwards: control 1+ objectives (excl. home) — 4VP

DELAYING ACTION (vs Purge the Foe)
  Any battle round: 1+ enemy units destroyed this turn — 2VP
  2nd round onwards: control 1+ objectives (excl. home) — 4VP; and 1+ expansion
    objectives — 3VP

OUTMANOEUVRE (vs Disruption — mirror matchup, x2 copies seen)
  Any battle round: control opponent's home objective — 10VP
  1st round: for each objective you control (excl. home) — 4VP
  2nd-3rd round: for each objective you control (excl. home) — 5VP
  4th round onwards: for each objective you control (excl. home/expansion) — 6VP

SMOKE AND MIRRORS (vs Reconnaissance, Objective Action)
  Any battle round: for each objective decoyed this turn — 2VP (+2VP cumulative if
    within opponent's territory)
  2nd round onwards: control 1+ objectives (excl. home) — 4VP
  End of battle: 1+ objectives are decoyed — 10VP

LOCATE AND DENY (vs Priority Assets)
  Start of turn: select one of your operation markers; check enemy units in the
    same terrain area as that marker (contest logic).
  Any battle round: 1+ enemy units that started the turn within range of an
    operation marker are destroyed — 4VP
  2nd round onwards: control 1+ objectives (excl. home) — 4VP
  End of battle: only one of your operation markers remains, uncontested — 5VP

Note: "Sensor Sweep" appeared at the edge of one Disruption photo but is NOT one
of Disruption's 5 — it was a stray card from an adjacent stack in frame (per
instruction to ignore edge/flipped cards). Not included above; likely belongs to
whichever disposition its own lower-left icon actually shows.

PURGE THE FOE (sword-in-triangle) — full 5-matchup set confirmed
================================================================================
Straightforward kill-count-driven missions, most with a control-based kicker.

DESTROYER'S WRATH (vs Priority Assets)
  Any battle round: 1+ enemy units destroyed this turn — 3VP
  2nd round onwards: control 1+ objectives (excl. home) — 4VP

CONSECRATE (vs Reconnaissance)
  Any battle round: 1-2 objectives consecrated this turn — 3VP; 3+ consecrated — 6VP
  2nd round onwards: control 1+ objectives (excl. home) — 4VP
  End of battle: opponent's home objective is consecrated — 5VP

PUNISHMENT (vs Disruption)
  Start of turn: select up to 3 enemy units on the battlefield/within range of
    objectives that destroyed a friendly unit last turn — they are "condemned"
    until the start of your next turn.
  Any battle round: 1+ condemned units destroyed this turn — 5VP
  2nd round onwards: control 1+ objectives (excl. home) — 4VP
  End of battle: control opponent's home objective — 8VP

MEATGRINDER (vs Purge the Foe — mirror matchup, x2 copies seen)
  Any battle round: 1+ enemy units destroyed this turn — 3VP
  2nd round onwards: control 1+ objectives (excl. home) — 4VP; more enemy units
    destroyed than friendly units lost last turn — 5VP

UNSTOPPABLE FORCE (vs Take and Hold)
  Any battle round: 1+ enemy units destroyed this turn — 3VP
  2nd round onwards: for each objective you did NOT control at the start of the
    turn (excl. home) — 3VP
  End of battle: control 1+ central objectives — 5VP

PRIORITY ASSETS (target/compass) — full 5-matchup set confirmed (front-of-card)
================================================================================
Earlier version of this section was built from card BACKS (action mechanics only,
lightning-bolt icon, no scoring) — corrected below using the actual fronts. VP
numbers below are best-effort reads off small print, flag anything that looks off.

SECURE ASSET (vs Take and Hold, Objective Action)
  Any battle round, end of turn: a friendly unit secured this objective this turn
    (see below) — 6VP; 1+ units that started the turn within 6" of a central
    objective were destroyed — 2VP
  2nd round onwards: control 1+ objectives — 4VP; 1+ of those is a central
    objective — cumulative bonus (~8VP, unconfirmed)
  End of battle: control opponent's home objective — 10VP

VITAL LINK (vs Purge the Foe, Objective Action)
  Any battle round: control 1+ central objectives — 2VP; for each of your
    operation markers within range of a central objective — +1VP cumulative
  2nd round onwards: control 1+ objectives — 4VP; 1+ central — cumulative bonus
    (unconfirmed)
  End of battle: 3+ central objectives — 10VP (unconfirmed)

EXTRACT RELIC (vs Disruption, Objective Action)
  Any battle round: 1+ of your operation markers within a terrain area — VP
    (value unconfirmed)
  2nd round onwards: control 1+ objectives — 4VP
  End of battle: only 1 of your opponent's operation markers on the battlefield,
    within a terrain area with one of yours and no enemy units present — ~4VP

VANGUARD OPERATION (vs Reconnaissance, Objective Action)
  Any battle round: a friendly unit performed a vanguard operation this turn —
    4VP; 1+ enemy units destroyed this turn — 2VP
  2nd round onwards: control 1+ objectives — 4VP
  End of battle: control opponent's home objective — 10VP

SABOTAGE (vs Priority Assets — mirror matchup, x2 copies seen, Objective Action)
  Any battle round: for each friendly unit that committed sabotage this turn —
    3VP; for each of those within range of an objective in opponent's
    territory — +2VP cumulative
  2nd round onwards: control 1+ objectives — 4VP

Action definitions (from the earlier back-of-card read, still accurate for HOW
each action works — just missing scoring context before):
  SECURE ASSET: starts your Shooting phase, 1 friendly unit within range of an
    objective (incl. home), once/turn, completes end of turn if unit controls it.
  VITAL LINK/MAINTAIN CONTROL: same shape, 1 friendly unit within range of an
    objective, once/turn.
  EXTRACT RELIC: same shape, effect places an operation marker within range of
    the objective.
  VANGUARD OPERATION: 1 friendly unit within opponent's territory, once/turn,
    completes if no enemy units are within that terrain area.
  SABOTAGE: unlimited uses/turn, each unit must target a different objective.

================================================================================
TAKE AND HOLD (shield/skull) — full 5-matchup set confirmed
================================================================================
Simple objective-count/control missions, least reliant on actions or kills.

INESCAPABLE DOMINION (vs Priority Assets)
  Any battle round, end of turn: control 2+ objectives — 4VP
  2nd round onwards: control 2+ objectives — 5VP
  End of battle: control opponent's home objective — 5VP

PURGE AND SECURE (vs Reconnaissance)
  Any battle round, end of turn: 1+ enemy units destroyed this turn within range of
    1+ objectives — 3VP
  2nd round onwards, end of Command phase (or end of turn in 5th round): for each
    objective you control (excl. home) — 4VP

DETERMINED ACQUISITION (vs Disruption)
  Any battle round, end of turn: 1+ objectives you did NOT control at start of
    turn (excl. home) — 2VP
  2nd round onwards: for each objective you control (excl. home) — +3VP cumulative

IMMOVABLE OBJECT (vs Purge the Foe)
  Any battle round, end of turn: control one central objective — 3VP
  2nd-4th round: control 1+ objectives (excl. home) — 5VP
  5th round: same — 5VP

BATTLEFIELD DOMINANCE (vs Take and Hold — mirror matchup, x2 copies seen)
  1st-2nd round, end of turn: control more objectives than opponent — 2VP
  2nd round onwards: for each objective you control (excl. home) — +2VP cumulative;
    bonus if you control your home objective

TAKEAWAYS FOR LIST-BUILDING (per faction conversation, not on the cards themselves)
================================================================================
- Recon rewards SPREAD (multiple quarters), MOBILITY, and ACTIONS over raw killing —
  favors more independent cheap/mobile units over concentrated blobs.
- Disruption leans on terrain control, decoys, and operation markers — action
  economy and board denial matter more than a kill count.
- Purge the Foe is the simplest "kill stuff, hold what you can" profile — favors
  raw damage output (GMND, Crowe+Purifiers) over spread.
- Priority Assets is pure objective-action completion, scoring mechanism not yet
  located (not on the cards themselves) — needs the core/event-companion rules.
- Take and Hold is the most "just control more objectives than them" of the five —
  favors durable, hard-to-shift units sitting on objectives over mobility.

Since our list targets Reconnaissance specifically: whichever of the 5 Recon
missions comes up depends on the OPPONENT's disposition — e.g. facing a Purge the
Foe opponent means playing Triangulation (an action/positioning mission), while
facing a Take and Hold opponent means Reconnaissance Sweep (quarters + kills). The
list may want to flex differently depending on what the opponent's disposition
tells you about which of the 5 Recon missions is actually in play that game.
