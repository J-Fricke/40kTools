CHAOS KNIGHTS - WAR DOG NOTES (11th ed)
Source: wahapedia wh40k11ed + MFM v1.3 + Faction Pack v1.2. Pulled 2026-09-02.

=== DETACHMENT STACKING (key point) ===
MFM v1.3 lists "UPDATED - UNIQUE TAG REMOVED" against BOTH Houndpack Lance
and Hunting Warpack. The "WAR DOGS unique tag" (still printed in Faction
Pack v1.2 text, "cannot be taken with another WAR DOGS detachment") is
REMOVED by MFM v1.3.
=> Houndpack Lance (2DP, Reconnaissance) + Hunting Warpack (1DP, Reconnaissance)
   CAN now be run together = 3DP.
Helhunt Lance (2DP, Disruption) - no tag noted either.

=== DETACHMENTS (MFM v1.3) ===
Houndpack Lance   2DP  Reconnaissance
  Rule "Marked Prey": start of Command phase pick 1 enemy unit; War Dog
  attacks vs it (if visible) get [SUSTAINED HITS 1] until next Command phase.
  Army needs 3+ War Dog units; all War Dogs gain Battleline; pick 3 War Dog
  units to gain CHARACTER (Enhancements / Warlord eligible).
  Enh: Final Howl 20 (friendly War Dogs w/in 6" reroll 1s to wound),
       Loping Predator 10 (bearer's ranged weapons gain [ASSAULT]),
       Panoply of the Cursed Knights 15 (attacks vs bearer worsen AP by 1),
       Preyslayer's Mantle 15 (text not pulled).
  Strat (post-v1.2 errata): Animalistic Rage 1CP (opp Shooting/Fight, just
       after a not-yet-activated War Dog is destroyed - free shoot/fight),
       Harrying Hounds 1CP (enemy ends move w/in 8" of a War Dog -> that
       War Dog moves 6"), Encircling Pack 1CP (end of enemy Fight phase,
       War Dog w/in 12" of board edge -> into reserves to outflank).

Hunting Warpack   1DP  Reconnaissance
  Rule "Scenting Fear": once per battle round, Shooting phase, pick 1 visible
  enemy unit w/in 12" of a friendly WAR DOG unit -> that unit +6" detection
  range (easier to hit / harder to hide).
  Enh: Soul-spoor Auspicator 15 (HUNTSMAN only, ranged attacks +6" R),
       Snarling Rivalry 10 (EXECUTIONER only, ranged attacks [IGNORES COVER]).
  Strat: Insensate Bloodthirst 1CP (Fight, enemy targets a War Dog -> FNP 5+),
       Leash of the Masters 1CP (after a War Dog shoots, still eligible to
       start an action), Stalking Focus 1CP (opp Shooting, War Dog on
       objective -> ranged attacks vs it -1 AP until that unit has attacked).

Helhunt Lance   2DP  Disruption
  Rule "Masters of the Pack": if a Titanic Chaos Knights unit has an Aura
  that affects friendly War Dogs, while 2+ friendly War Dog models are in
  range of that Aura the Titanic Knight is ALSO affected by it.

Iconoclast Fiefdom  1DP  Take and Hold
  Rule "Wretched Thralls": may include up to 500pts of DAMNED units
  (Codex: CSM); they reroll Leadership.

=== CK CHASSIS DEFENCE COMPARISON (Faction Pack v1.2, all verified) ===
Every non-Acastus CK Knight is T11. Differences are save / wounds / move.
  Lancer      T11 W28 3+  4++ ALL-ROUND   M14"   <- best invuln in the index
  Atrapos     T11 W28 3+  5++ all-round   M12"
  Magaera     T11 W26 3+  5++ all-round   M10"
  Styrix      T11 W26 3+  5++ all-round   M10"
  Castigator  T11 W28 3+  5++ RANGED ONLY M12"   (no melee invuln)
  Acheron     T11 W28 3+  5++ RANGED ONLY M12"
  Desecrator / Rampager / Abominant / Despoiler / Tyrant / Ruinator:
              T11 W28 3+  5++ RANGED ONLY M12"   (no melee invuln)
  Acastus Asterius / Porphyrion: T13 W30 2+ 5++ RANGED ONLY M8" (725-860pts)
=> The Cerastus Lancer is the single tankiest CK chassis vs mixed/melee
   damage: only chassis with an always-on all-round 4++. BUT all knights are
   T11 (premium S12+ AT wounds them on 3s regardless), and a 4++/5++ does
   nothing vs Devastating Wounds / mortal wounds (Necrons, Deathwing/Custodes
   fists, Lion/DA). "Tankiest model" != "best passive objective holder" -
   the Lancer is a melee piece (12" gun only) and taxes the opponent only as
   a live charge threat.
NOTE: earlier assumption that Magaera is the tankiest CK chassis was WRONG
(10th-ed carryover). In 11th, Magaera is W26 / 5++, strictly worse defence
than the Lancer's W28 / 4++.

=== "SECOND BIG" CANDIDATES (if adding one, dropping ~3 Dogs) ===
Atrapos 385  M12 T11 W28 3+/5++ all-round. Damaged 1-10. Deadly Demise D6+2.
  Lascutter hi-int 24" D6 S14 AP-3 D4 [Sus1]; graviton singularity cannon
  24" D3 S16 AP-4 D6+1 [BLAST] (+DEV WOUNDS/HAZARDOUS in singularity mode).
  Macro-extinction: +1 hit vs MONSTER/VEHICLE, +1 wound too vs TITANIC/TOWERING.
  Aura: friendly War Dogs w/in 6" reroll hits vs Titanic/Towering.
  => dedicated enemy-knight / big-vehicle / monster DELETER at range. Weak
     into MEQ/infantry (low shot count). Best "second big" for this list:
     fills the ranged anti-big gap, Cerastus chassis, synergy aura.
Magaera 375  M10 T11 W26 3+/5++ all-round. Damaged 1-9. Deadly Demise D6.
  Repair Auto-simulacra: regain D3 lost W end of Command phase.
  Lightning cannon 48" 12A S9 AP0 D2 [Sus2] (AP0 = weak); melee reaper
  chainsword or swap to hekaton claw (S20 AP-3 D8).
  => tankier ONLY via regen, and only in an attrition grind (regen ~= +2W/
     turn, ~0 vs being focused from full in one turn). Worse guns, slower,
     melee profile redundant with the Lancer. Different chassis (Abhorrent).
     Pick only as a Take-and-Hold melee grind anchor, not for this list.
Styrix 365   same hull as Magaera. Volkite chierovile 30" 12A S12 AP0 D3
  [DEV WOUNDS] + graviton crusher [ANTI-VEHICLE 2+] + grav-pin (slow enemy
  infantry). Better shooting Questoris than Magaera but still AP0, M10, no regen.
Abominant 355 (1st-2nd)  M10 T11 W26 3+/5++(ranged only)  Damaged 1-9. DD D6.
  Volkite combustor 30" 12A S12 AP0 D3 [IGNORES COVER, DEVASTATING WOUNDS].
  Vortex Terrors (Psychic): start of Shooting, force a Battle-shock test on
  a unit within 24".  Warp Storms (Psychic): end of Movement, D6 per enemy
  unit within 9", 3+ = D3 mortal wounds.  Melee: electroscourge 9A S10 AP-2 D3.
  => only CK knight with BOTH Dev Wounds and repeatable mortal-wound output;
     the answer to invuln/FNP/reanimation-heavy metas (Custodes, Deathwing,
     Necrons). 30" range = medium, not a true gunline piece. No War Dog aura.

Knight Tyrant 400/420  M8 T12 W28 3+/5++(ranged only)  Damaged 1-10. DD D6+2.
  Brimstone volcano lance 60" D3 S20 AP-5 D6+8 [BLAST]; 2x gheiststrike
  missile 72" 1 S12 AP-6 D6+1 [ANTI-TITANIC 4+, DEV WOUNDS]; ectoplasma
  decimator 36" D6+3 S8 AP-3 D2 (supercharge S9 AP-4 D3 HAZARDOUS); 2x twin
  daemonbreath meltagun 12" MELTA 2; twin Desecrator cannon 36" D6 S6 chaff.
  Infernal Aegis (Aura): friendly War Dog w/in 6" has Benefit of Cover (defensive).
  Bastion of Corruption: enemy Reinforcements can't set up within 12" (anti-deep-strike).
  => real gunline anchor + the only in-faction deep-strike denial. But M8",
     no melee invuln, 400pts, huge target, static in a mobile Recon list.

BASTIONS OF TYRANNY (1DP, Priority Assets): mono-KNIGHT TYRANT detachment.
  Rule only buffs Knight Tyrant, and only vs battle-shocked targets. Taking
  it forces dropping Hunting Warpack (3DP cap). NOT worth it - if you want a
  Tyrant, run it inside Houndpack + Hunting Warpack with no dedicated detach.

Verdict: Atrapos > Magaera > Styrix for a "back-field big that shoots" role.
For an invuln/FNP-heavy meta, Abominant beats all of them. Tyrant if the
problem is kiting gunlines + deep strike.
Still: two knights that both must walk into a shooting meta = worst case;
only add a second big if it's the RANGED Atrapos.

AURA CAVEAT: every big knight (and the Lancer, Dark Fervour = Assault) carries
a "friendly War Dog w/in 6\" gets X" aura, so a second big DOES force-multiply
nearby Dogs. But: 6" bubble only (2-3 Dogs at most), it competes with the
Lancer's own aura, and it pulls Dogs into a cluster which fights the Recon
plan (Dogs want to spread to score). Atrapos aura (reroll hits vs Titanic/
Towering) is best ONLY vs enemy Titanic (enemy knights, Gorkanaut/Stompa,
big Tau) - dead vs pure infantry armies. It's a datasheet ability, not a
detachment payoff, so the "2DP of rules only touch Dogs" point still holds.

=== RECONNAISSANCE DISPOSITION (11th) ===
Primaries reward board presence / positioning, NOT kills: score for holding
objectives OUTSIDE your deployment zone each round, being in the outer
board sections, doing actions in set spots, grabbing an objective round 1.
Wants fast MSU that can fan out, do an action, and leave. Favours War Dog
count over a second big Knight. (Kills only matter via Purge the Foe.)
